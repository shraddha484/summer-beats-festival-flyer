# summer-beats-festival-flyer


Title: 🎶 Summer Beats Festival 2025 – Vibrant Music Event Flyer

Description:
This project presents a vibrant and energetic flyer design created for the Summer Beats Festival 2025 — a celebration of electronic music, color, and rhythm. The design features neon gradients, fluid abstract shapes, and dynamic typography to reflect the pulse of a live music experience. Ideal for promoting music festivals, DJ events, or nightlife parties, this flyer captures the essence of sound and summer energy.

Key Highlights:

Bold, futuristic typography with dynamic layout 🎧

Neon-inspired color palette with abstract wave visuals 🌈

Eye-catching placement of artist lineup and event details 🎵

High-contrast callouts for date, venue, and time 🔥

Designed for print and digital promotion across platforms 📲
